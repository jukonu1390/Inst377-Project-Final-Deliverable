About This App


This Anime Recommender web application helps users discover new anime to watch. With thousands of anime available online, users may struggle to find something new to watch. This app helps with that by allowing users to search for anime, get recommendations, and save their favorite shows.


Data is sourced from MyAnimeList through the Jikan public API.
